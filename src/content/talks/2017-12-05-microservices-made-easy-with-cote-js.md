The concept of microservices is extremely hot. Unfortunately, we see a lot of ideas from the past rebranded as microservices; whereas the thinking behind microservices imply and promise a bigger change. This talk will go over the details of what actually makes a microservices architecture and how other distributed systems — that rely on queues and other mechanisms to function — fail to fulfill the promise.

A modern microservices implementation should be;

- Zero-configuration
- Highly-redundant
- Fault-tolerant
- Self-healing
- Auto-discovery

We will specifically focus on such an implementation with Docker and cote, a Node.js library.
