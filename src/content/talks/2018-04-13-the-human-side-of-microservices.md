<blockquote class="pull">

organizations which design systems … are constrained to produce designs which are copies of the communication structures of these organizations

</blockquote>

<blockquote class="pull">

— Melvin Conway, 1967

</blockquote>

Microservices is an architectural shift, a huge topic that demands change in every aspect of software delivery. It is also more than a technical problem — your microservices architecture can be as solid and efficient as your team communication. This talk tries to describe the human aspect of implementing a microservices approach, and how this affects team communication.
