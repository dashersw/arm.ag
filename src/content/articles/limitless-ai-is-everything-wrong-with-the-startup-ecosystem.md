I loved my [@LimitlessAI](https://x.com/LimitlessAI) pendant. It was love at first sight. Even though I was terrified by their former product Rewind, and their founder [@dsiroker](https://x.com/dsiroker) always looked like something is off with him, the product and the service was impeccable.

I ordered my Pendant on April 16th 2024, thinking it would arrive at the end of 2024. It shipped only in May 2025, after more than a year!

I still loved it...

Until they got bought by Meta and killed it two days ago.

Now their privacy policy is basically garbage. Initially they promised to not use customer data for training (duh), even opening their privacy policy with the sentences: "committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. Your trust & privacy is more important to us than anything else. And that is why we will never sell your data or use your data for advertising."

... and then they sold everyone's data to Meta. I haven't agreed to the new privacy policy yet, but I don't know if they already stole my data. The new privacy policy basically says they can do anything with your data.

This is just so annoying... I knew this company could not be trusted, and here we are.

Another issue is, of course, that I paid for a hardware believing it would work for years to come, and bought a 1-year subscription for $348 only 4.5 months ago... after they had claimed "[the device] is $99 for a limited time. We're proud to share it does not require a subscription." Of course it required a subscription to function properly.

And now that I checked my emails again, I saw that it's not usable in the EU anymore! So they immediately killed the device and the app!

I was wondering why there was a glitch today when I tried turning the device on. This is seriously unbelievable. Unfathomable...

Let's see how we can fight for a refund, save our data, and build an alternative service... I'm so happy I built [hirecasey.ai](https://hirecasey.ai/) in early 2023.
