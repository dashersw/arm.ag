Before GitHub there was Planet Source Code, a site where programmers uploaded snippets and whole projects for others to read, rate and reuse. That is where I published my first open source code, as a teenager, under the handle I still use everywhere: DasherSW.

The Internet Archive still has my author page with two Visual Basic submissions:

- **Moving Caption** (August 1999, VB 5 and 6). It scrolls a window's caption from left to right; the text leaves from the right edge and comes back in from the left. Thirty-six people rated it.
- **DasherSW's Rectangular Marquee Tool** (August 2000, VB 5 and 6). The description I wrote then: “Ever wondered how you draw rectangles to select units in strategy games? Well here's the solution”, bundled with helper code that went from moving a form without a caption to alpha blending.
