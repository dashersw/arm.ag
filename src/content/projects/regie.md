Regie is a tiny state management library for applications that don't use a framework. You mutate a plain object, and the parts of your app that observe it are notified. There are no actions, reducers or setters.

The reactivity model in regie is the direct ancestor of the stores in [Gea](/projects/gea/). I introduced it in the talk [Reactivity in Vanilla JS with Regie](/talks/2024-04-01-reactivity-in-vanilla-js-with-regie-a-new-state-of-play/).
